Approach

Mentoring values

What I try to bring to supervision

Clarity
Making the problem understandable.
  • Define the question precisely.
  • Separate ambition from scope.
  • Keep the work legible.
Method
Building work on a solid process.
  • Research logic and evidence.
  • Prototype-test-iterate rhythm.
  • Connection between concept and implementation.
Technical grounding
Connecting ideas to working systems.
  • XR interfaces and tools.
  • AI / IoT implementation awareness.
  • System architecture thinking.
Human-centered perspective
Why the work should matter.
  • Usability and user interpretation.
  • Context of use and real workflows.
  • Design responsibility.
Communication
Helping students explain their own work.
  • Presentations and academic writing.
  • Visual explanation of systems.
  • Reasoned design choices.
Steady progress
Turning uncertainty into manageable steps.
  • Milestones and regular feedback.
  • Iterative refinement.
  • Practical research momentum.
