Approach

How I supervise

I prefer clear framing, realistic milestones, iterative feedback and alignment between research goals and implementation.

  • Define the question precisely.
  • Separate ambition from scope.
  • Turn uncertainty into manageable steps.
Domains

Areas I can supervise

Supervision can cover Mixed Reality, digital twins, human-centered design, AI-supported systems and monitoring technologies.

  • AR / VR interaction and prototyping.
  • Digital twins and industrial interfaces.
  • XR for rehabilitation, training and usability.
Method

Support that connects concept and build

Supervision is strongest when conceptual reasoning and implementation evolve together.

  • Research logic and evidence.
  • Prototype-test-iterate rhythm.
  • Connection between idea and system.
Communication

Helping students explain their work

Students often need support not only building the system, but explaining why the system matters and how it should be read.

  • Academic writing and structure.
  • Visual explanation of systems.
  • Presenting design logic and evidence.
Human-centered perspective

Keeping users and context in view

Supervision should keep the human side alive even when the project is technically deep.

  • Usability and user interpretation.
  • Context of use.
  • Responsible design choices.
Technical grounding

Research that can actually run

Guided work benefits from strong technical grounding in tools, architectures and implementation choices.

  • XR tools and engineering awareness.
  • AI / IoT implementation logic.
  • System architecture thinking.
Project rhythm

Steady progress instead of chaos

A good supervision process gives the work structure, momentum and realistic checkpoints.

  • Milestones and review points.
  • Iterative refinement.
  • Clarity under time pressure.
