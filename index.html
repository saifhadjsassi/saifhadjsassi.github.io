RESEARCH THREADS

What ties the projects together

Whether the context is industrial, medical, educational or environmental, I work on systems where sensing, data, AI and immersive interfaces must work together as a coherent whole.

  • Mixed Reality interfaces for operational tasks and training.
  • Digital twins that support decision-making and interaction.
  • IoT and AI pipelines that turn data into usable insight.
CURRENT WORK

LIST — Mixed Reality for hazard training and digital twins

At LIST, I have worked first as Research Engineer in Mixed Reality and now as Postdoctoral Researcher in Mixed Reality.

  • RISARX — enhanced UX and interface work for training first responders handling radiological incidents.
  • CACTUS — AR interfaces for wagon and stage management within digital twins.
  • STARRI — AR systems for hazard training, tracking physical objects and radiation emissions.
IMMERSIVE SAFETY

ImmerSAFE, rehabilitation, and safety-oriented XR

My work includes immersive training for firefighters and VR applications for upper-limb rehabilitation and therapy-oriented evaluation.

  • Leonardo / ImmerSAFE — simulation-based AR and VR training for firefighters.
  • Upper Limb Rehab — VR applications developed with Roma Tre and University Campus Bio-Medico di Roma.
  • Research around design guidelines, performance evaluation and validation of immersive XR systems.
AI • IOT • MONITORING

Air quality, intelligent monitoring, and cognitive architectures

Another major line of my research focuses on indoor air-quality monitoring, immersive visualization, communication technologies and AI-based data solutions.

  • IoT systems for indoor air-quality monitoring and assessment.
  • Deep learning and AR for prediction and visualization workflows.
  • Architectures for cognitive IoT, data integration and analytics.
