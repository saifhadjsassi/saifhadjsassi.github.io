Industrial DT

CACTUS

Developing advanced AR interfaces for wagon movement and stage management inside digital twins.

  • LIST and Waagner Biro collaboration.
  • AR interfaces for movement and coordination.

Hazard XR

RISARX

Improving user experience for hazard training, physical tracking and radiation-emission visualization.

  • Radiological and first-responder contexts.
  • Physical-object tracking and situational awareness.
  • AR workflows for training and operational support.
Immersive training

ImmerSAFE

Simulation-based VR training for firefighters developed with Leonardo and Roma Tre.

  • First-responder training.
  • VR scenarios for stressful environments.
  • Marie Curie framework.
Rehabilitation

Upper Limb Rehab

Immersive applications for collecting and visualizing upper-limb movement in rehabilitation contexts.

  • Quest 2-based evaluation.
  • Clinical and therapy-oriented workflows.
  • XR feedback for movement understanding.
Monitoring

IAQ and gas plant systems

Projects linking IoT, visualization and deep learning for air quality and industrial monitoring.

  • Indoor air-quality AR visualization.
  • Gas-pipe data with LoRaWAN sensors.
  • Cognitive IoT and digital-twin logic.
Accessibility

AR for visually impaired people

Architectures for recognizing text and objects to improve navigation and accessibility.

  • Arabic text visualization.
  • AR-assisted perception.
  • Accessible spatial information.
Deep learning

Computer vision systems

Deep-learning work on Arabic scene-text detection and related visual problems.

  • Fully convolutional networks.
  • Arabic scene-text datasets and pipelines.
  • Vision methods in applied contexts.

Project philosophy

The projects change domain but not logic: understand a real problem, build the right technical system, then design an interface people can actually use.

That is why the same portfolio can span safety, industry, rehabilitation, accessibility and monitoring without feeling fragmented.
